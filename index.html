Mooncat Vault & Inventory User Guide

Everything you need to know about browsing, managing your personal collection, wishlisting, and using every button.

✨ 1. How to Manage Your Own Inventory

1
Create or Switch Your Profile:

At the top-right, click + New Profile to create your own collection (e.g. "Emma's Vault"). It starts completely blank (0 owned) and is 100% private to your browser.

2
Add Colors You Own:

Find any polish in the Main Catalog and click + Add to Owned. Or go to My Orders & Inventory and click + Add / Import Orders to paste your Mooncat receipt!

3
Track Quantities & Duplicates:

In the My Orders & Inventory tab, use the + and - buttons to adjust your physical bottles on hand if you buy backups or gift one away.

4
Wishlist Shades You Want:

Click ✦ Wishlist on shades you desire. (The app prevents you from accidentally adding colors you already own!) Once purchased, click 🛍 Mark Purchased to move it to your collection.

🎛️ 2. What Every Button Does

Button Where it is What it does
+ New Profile Top Header Creates a brand new, empty personal collection. Keeps everyone's inventory completely separate without mixing up bottles.
Profile Dropdown Top Header Switches between different collections saved on this device (e.g. Melissa's Vault vs. a friend's vault).
⚡ Check New Releases Main Catalog Toolbar Queries Mooncat.com live to detect newly dropped shades or collections and flags them with a glowing ✨ NEW badge.
⬇ Download CSV Main Catalog / Orders Exports your collection or the entire catalog as a clean spreadsheet file that opens in Excel, Google Sheets, or Apple Numbers.
🎴 Cards / 📋 Table Main Catalog Toolbar Toggles the view between visual photo cards (with high-res nail swatches) and a compact spreadsheet table.
+ Add to Owned Polish Cards & Rows Adds 1 bottle to your inventory, marks it as Owned (Y), and automatically removes it from your Wishlist if it was there.
✦ Wishlist Polish Cards & Rows Saves a color to your Wishlist. Notice: this button is disabled on shades you already own so you never buy duplicates by mistake!
🛍 Mark Purchased Wishlist Tab When you buy a shade from your wishlist, clicking this immediately removes it from your wishlist and adds it to your inventory!
+ / - Buttons My Orders Tab Increments or decrements your physical bottle count on hand (for backup bottles or gifts).
+ Add / Import Orders My Orders Tab Opens a window where you can paste order confirmations or a list of polish names. The app matches them automatically!

📑 3. The 3 Tabs

  • ✨ Main Catalog: Mooncat's full catalog of colors and sets with photos, finishes, color palettes, official descriptions, and your Owned (Y/N) status. Filter by finish, color, new releases, or retired shades.
  • 📦 My Orders & Inventory: Your personal alphabetical list of ordered shades, showing your total ordered count, physical bottles on hand, and notes.
  • 💫 Wishlist: Your dream list of unowned shades and sets to buy next. Move them to your inventory in 1 click once purchased.

🏷️ 4. What the Status Badges Mean

In Stock Available to purchase directly on mooncat.com.
Out of Stock Temporarily sold out, expected to be restocked.
⚰️ DEARLY DEPARTED Retired from Mooncat's line forever. No longer restocked.
✨ NEW Recently dropped shade from Mooncat's newest collections.
Owned: 1 You have this polish in your personal collection.
Wishlist You've marked this unowned shade to buy next.
